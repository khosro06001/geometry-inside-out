# Geometry from the Inside Out

### A visual construction revealing the dot product as a geometric area, with implications for the structure of mathematics.

**K. Pourkavoos, 2026**

---

## Summary

A point in geometry is isomorphic to infinity in algebra.

Zero in algebra is isomorphic to an infinite dimensional object in geometry.

## The Starting Point

Take a triangle. Express its three edges as vectors **a**, **b**, **c** satisfying:

**a** + **b** + **c** = **0**

Build a square on each side. Each square turns out to be partitioned into exactly two rectangles — and the area of each rectangle is the negative of a pairwise dot product of the edge vectors.

This is not a numerical coincidence. It is a structural truth about every triangle.

The included diagram makes it visible.

## What Follows from This

Once you see the dot product as a geometric area living inside the triangle, a cascade of ideas follows:

- **The Law of Cosines** falls out in two lines — no auxiliary constructions, no dropped altitudes. It is revealed as a dot product identity, not a trigonometric theorem.

- **The Pythagorean theorem** is the special case where one dot product vanishes (right angle), and one rectangle collapses to zero.

- **The Pourkavoos Invariant** — for any triangle with edge vectors **a**, **b**, **c**:

  > −(**a**·**b** + **b**·**c** + **c**·**a**) = a² − **b**·**c** = b² − **c**·**a** = c² − **a**·**b**

  The area of the square on any side, minus the dot product of the other two sides, is the same for all three sides. A triangle invariant not previously named in the standard literature.

- **The dimensional hierarchy inverts.** The dot product takes two 1D objects and produces a 0D scalar — but that scalar *is* a 2D area. This suggests that areas, not points, are the natural starting objects of geometry.

- **Intersection as the generative operation.** A point is the intersection of two lines. A line is the intersection of two planes. A plane is the intersection of two volumes — like the septum between two soap bubbles. Objects do not exist prior to their relationships; they exist *because* of them.

- **Zero and infinity are structural twins.** The intersection hierarchy descends toward zero dimension, where the operation breaks down — just as counting ascends toward infinity, where arithmetic breaks down. A point is to geometry what infinity is to arithmetic: not a starting primitive but an unreachable limit, approached from above but never truly arrived at.

## Connections to Other Mathematics and Physics

The construction connects, sometimes surprisingly, to:

- **Projective geometry** — where points are defined as intersections of lines, not as primitives
- **Quantum mechanics** — where probability is an inner product, and existence is relational
- **Category theory** — where objects are defined entirely by their relationships, not by internal structure
- **The Riemann sphere** — where zero and infinity are formally dual, related by the map z → 1/z
